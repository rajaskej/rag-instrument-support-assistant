Overview

The DM-5400 is a mid-range digital density meter designed for high-precision liquid characterization in laboratory and quality control environments. Combining an oscillating U-tube measurement core with automated viscosity correction, the DM-5400 compensates for viscous damping effects across the entire operational range to deliver accurate, reproducible density values without manual correction factors.

Key Features

  • Automated Viscosity Correction: Real-time damping analysis eliminates viscosity-induced bias up to 700 mPa·s.
  • Integrated Peltier Thermostat: Fast, stable electronic temperature regulation without external liquid baths.
  • Optical Bubble Detection: High-resolution digital imaging of the measuring cell allows real-time inspection for bubbles or filling anomalies.
  • Compliance & Data Integrity: Supports audit trails, user access control, and electronic signatures compliant with 21 CFR Part 11.
  • Custom Method Storage: Pre-programmed scales for Brix, API gravity, alcohol concentration, and custom concentration formulas.
