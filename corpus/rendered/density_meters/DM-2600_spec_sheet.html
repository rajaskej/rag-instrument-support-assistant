DM-2600 Product Overview

The DM-2600 is an entry-level benchtop digital density meter engineered for routine quality control, education, and general industrial laboratory applications. Combining oscillating U-tube technology with an intuitive touchscreen interface, the DM-2600 offers fast, reliable determination of liquid density, specific gravity, and concentration tables (Brix, API, alcohol, and user-defined scales).

Key Features

  • High-Contrast Touch Interface: 7-inch color display with step-by-step guidance for calibration, measurement, and cleaning routines.
  • Integrated Peltier Thermostat: Eliminates the need for external water baths, maintaining precise sample temperature.
  • Bubble Detection Technology: Real-time optical inspection helps prevent errors caused by micro-bubbles in the measuring cell.
  • Compact Footprint: Space-saving benchtop chassis designed to maximize available laboratory workspace.
  • Data Management & Export: Storage for up to 5,000 measurements; export via USB (CSV/PDF) or direct connection to standard lab printers.
