# Overview

The DM-2600 is an entry-level benchtop digital density meter designed for routine laboratory analysis of liquid samples. Operating on the oscillating U-tube principle, the instrument provides accurate and repeatable density determinations across chemical, food and beverage, and petrochemical applications. 

# Specifications

| Parameter | Value |
| :--- | :--- |
| Density range | 0 to 3 g/cm3 |
| Accuracy | ±0.0001 g/cm3 |
| Temperature range | 15 to 70 C |
| Sample volume | 1.5 mL |

# Calibration Procedure

1. Switch on the instrument and permit a 15 minute warm-up period to ensure internal thermal equilibrium.
2. Flush the measuring cell three consecutive times using degassed distilled water.
3. Introduce the dry air reference into the cell and log the first calibration point.
4. Introduce the distilled water reference into the cell and log the second calibration point.
5. Verify that the measured calibration deviation is within 0.00005 g/cm3, then confirm and save the calibration settings.

# Troubleshooting

| Symptom | Error Code |
| :--- | :--- |
| Reading drifts upward slowly during a measurement | E-201 |
| Sample leaks at injection port or fails to fill the cell | E-101 |

# Error Codes

## E-101

**Description:** Sample injection failure.  
**Cause:** Syringe seal worn or improperly seated.  
**Resolution:** Inspect and replace the syringe seal, then re-inject the sample.

## E-201

**Description:** Temperature stabilization timeout.  
**Cause:** Thermostat connection loose or ambient temperature outside operating range.  
**Resolution:** Check the thermostat cable connection and ensure ambient temperature is between 15 and 35 C.
